

SKILLS IN OSK
	Skills in OSK are largely identical to D&D 5E, with a few adjustments.
	Note that Skills in OSK ARE NOT coupled to Attributes.
	The GM determines which Skill is appropriate for a given check.
	Additionally, if a Skill is roleplayed in a particularly clever way, the GM may grant advantage on the check. (Note that Persuasion is a special case in this regard - see below.)
	If a task does not easily fit into one of the given Skill categories, the GM may call for a check with an Attribute modifier instead.
	Skills in OSK are intentionally broad, and may achieve more than their standard 5E equivalents.
	For example, an Arcana check might identify a magical effect, or reveal whether an object is enchanted.

ACROBATICS:
	Acrobatics represents balance, flexibility, coordination, and controlled movement.
	Use it to balance on a narrow surface, perform a difficult landing, tumble through an obstacle, or escape certain restraints.

ANIMAL HANDLING:
	Animal Handling represents your ability to understand, calm, train, ride, or direct animals.
	Use it to soothe a frightened mount, interpret an animal's behavior, or persuade a wild creature not to attack.

ARCANA:
	Arcana represents knowledge and practical understanding of magic, enchantments, supernatural forces, and magical creatures.
	Use it to identify a spell, examine an enchanted object, interpret magical symbols, or detect nearby magical influence.

ATHLETICS:
	Athletics represents physical training, strength, endurance, and forceful movement.
	Use it to climb, swim, jump, grapple, shove, break an object, or perform another feat of physical power.

DECEPTION:
	Deception represents your ability to conceal the truth, mislead others, impersonate someone, or maintain a false story.
	Use it to lie convincingly, disguise your intentions, create a distraction, or pass yourself off as someone else.
	At the GM's discretion, Deception Checks can follow the same special rules as Persuasion Checks (see below).

HISTORY:
	History represents knowledge of past events, cultures, nations, wars, rulers, and important figures.
	Use it to recall historical information, recognize an ancient symbol, or understand the significance of a location or artifact.

INSIGHT:
	Insight represents your ability to interpret emotions, intentions, behavior, and social cues.
	Use it to recognize deception, determine someone's mood, anticipate their likely response, or understand an unspoken motive.

INTIMIDATION:
	Intimidation represents your ability to influence others through fear, threats, force, or overwhelming presence.
	Use it to frighten an opponent, compel cooperation, or discourage someone from taking action.

INVESTIGATION:
	Investigation represents careful examination, deduction, research, and the discovery of concealed information.
	Use it to search an area, find hidden objects, reconstruct events, analyze clues, or uncover the workings of a trap.

MEDICINE:
	Medicine represents knowledge of injury, illness, anatomy, treatment, and first aid.
	Use it to restore HP through the First Aid action.
	Use it to diagnose a condition, stabilize an injured creature, or identify a poison or disease.

NATURE:
	Nature represents knowledge of plants, animals, weather, ecosystems, and natural phenomena.
	Use it to identify a creature or plant, predict weather, understand an environment, or recognize a natural hazard.

PERFORMANCE:
	Performance represents your ability to entertain, captivate, or emotionally affect an audience.
	Use it to sing, dance, act, play an instrument, tell a story, or deliver a dramatic speech.

RELIGION:
	Religion represents knowledge of gods, faiths, rituals, sacred traditions, divine forces, and supernatural beliefs.
	Use it to identify religious symbols, recall doctrine, pray for divine aid, perform a ritual, or understand the behavior of a divine or unholy creature.

SLEIGHT OF HAND:
	Sleight of Hand represents precise manual coordination, concealment, and subtle manipulation.
	Use it to pick a lock, pickpocket a passerby, hide a small object, perform a hand trick, palm an item, or manipulate a delicate mechanism unnoticed.

STEALTH:
	Stealth represents your ability to avoid detection through silence, concealment, timing, or camouflage.
	Use it to move quietly, hide, follow someone unseen, or conceal signs of your presence.

SURVIVAL:
	Survival represents practical experience with wilderness travel, tracking, navigation, shelter, and environmental danger.
	Use it to follow tracks, avoid becoming lost, locate food or water, anticipate an ambush, or endure dangerous terrain.

ENGINEERING:
	Engineering represents knowledge of machines, structures, construction, traps, and mechanical systems.
	Use it to repair machinery, assess a bridge's stability, disable a mechanical trap, or build a contraption.

LANGUAGE:
	Language represents knowledge of spoken, written, signed, coded, and ancient forms of communication.
	By default, characters understand Common (i.e. the language understood almost universally) and any language associated with their Species.
	Use Language to translate unfamiliar writing, understand a related language, interpret a code, or communicate across a language barrier.
	A character with a +12 or +15 Language modifier is effectively a polyglot.

[SPECIAL SKILL] PERSUASION:
	Persuasion represents your minimum competence in reasoned or charismatic social influence.
	Use it to persuade, impress, negotiate, haggle, flirt, or request cooperation.
	Your Persuasion modifier is the minimum modifier added to a Persuasion Check.
	However, if a player engages in impressive, clever, or dramatic roleplaying, the GM may grant a higher modifier (from +3 to +15).
	Additionally, note that a successful Persuasion Check cannot still force another character to act irrationally or against deeply held convictions.
